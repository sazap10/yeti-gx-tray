# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person on a KDE Plasma desktop (Wayland) with a Logitech Yeti GX on USB, in a call or game, glancing at the system tray. The icon is read at 22 px next to other tray icons; interaction is click (mute), scroll (gain), right-click menu (Smart Audio Lock).

## Product Purpose

Make the mic's hardware gain, mute and Smart Audio Lock state visible and controllable without G HUB. The gain was invisible to Linux audio tools, which hid a "knob turned down" problem. Success: at a glance, the user knows both the gain and whether they are muted, and is never muted by accident.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, exact, native. A utility that disappears into the tray.

## Anti-references

- Loud, fully saturated colour on inactive/resting states.

## Design Principles

1. Gain and mute state carry equal weight; neither may be harder to read than the other.
2. State is never conveyed by colour alone.
3. Sit comfortably among the other tray icons; do not shout at rest.
4. Truthful over decorative: the icon shows what the hardware reports, including "disconnected".

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 for text-like glyphs, 3:1 for graphics). No state may rely on colour alone.
